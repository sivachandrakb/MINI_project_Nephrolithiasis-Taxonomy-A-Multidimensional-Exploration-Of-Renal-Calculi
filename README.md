# 🏥 Nephrolithiasis Taxonomy: A Multifaceted Exploration of Renal Calculi  

📌 **Authors**: Lakshmi V, Vb, Kb Sivachandra, S. Abhishek, T. Anjali  
📅 **Conference**: 2023 2nd International Conference on Automation, Computing, and Renewable Systems (**ICACRS**)  
📜 **Published by**: IEEE  
📄 **DOI**: [Link to Paper](10.1109/ICACRS58579.2023.10404959)  

---

## 📌 Abstract  
This study presents a **comprehensive taxonomy for nephrolithiasis (kidney stones)** using **advanced image classification and machine learning techniques**. The paper explores various **renal calculi types**, their **medical significance**, and **diagnostic approaches**. By leveraging **deep learning**, the study aims to enhance the accuracy of **automated kidney stone classification**, aiding in **early detection and treatment planning**.  

---

## 🚀 Key Contributions  
✅ **Taxonomy for Nephrolithiasis** – Categorizing different renal calculi types  
✅ **Deep Learning Model** – Custom CNN architecture for kidney stone classification  
✅ **Medical Imaging Analysis** – Insights into feature extraction from CT scans  
✅ **Performance Evaluation** – Comparative study of various ML models  

---

## 🛠 Methodology  
1. **Dataset Collection** – Kidney stone images from CT scans  
2. **Preprocessing** – Image augmentation & noise reduction  
3. **Model Development** – CNN-based classification model  
4. **Evaluation Metrics** – Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score  
5. **Comparison** – Traditional ML vs. Deep Learning approaches  

---

## 📊 Results & Findings  
- The proposed **CNN model** achieved a significant improvement in **classification accuracy**.  
- The study demonstrated the **effectiveness of deep learning over traditional ML methods**.  
- The findings emphasize the **importance of automated diagnostic tools in nephrology**.
